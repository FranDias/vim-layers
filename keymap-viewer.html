The USB half's screen shows the layer, a cheat line, WPM and Caps. The other screen shows the vim mode and the command typed so far. Key backlight lights only the keys that do something on the active layer.

Base

Standard ErgoDox QWERTY with macOS mods on the thumbs. Esc lives in the Caps Lock position: tap sends Esc and enters vim normal mode, hold is momentary. SYM and CMUX toggle on a tap and are momentary on a hold. LCD backlight white.

Sym

Tap or hold either inner key on the QWERTY row. F1–F12 on the number row, brackets and symbols under the left hand, a numpad under the right, media on the right thumb. LCD blue.

Vim

A modal engine on top of macOS editing keys. Counts work: 23e, 10j, 3dd. Operators d c y take a motion, doubled for a line; iw is the word under the cursor. i a I A o O s S c C drop back to insert. v V start visual modes. Ctrl+d/u page. Hold Esc in normal mode to leave without inserting. LCD green, amber in visual.

Cmux

Hold the top inner key on the left half. H J K L focus panes, [ ] cycle tabs, , . cycle workspaces, 1–9 jump to a workspace, D S split, T new tab, Z zoom, P palette. Thumbs send Esc and Shift+Tab to the agent. LCD orange.
